Our Mission

Serve people with excellence. Everything else follows.

The right lumber, when needed, at a fair price for all involved in the process.

Missions Lumber Company supplies a wide range of Western Wood species including Douglas Fir-Larch, Hem-Fir, and Spruce-Pine-Fir in all grades from utility to visual (#1&Btr, Select Structural) to machine stress-rated (MSR) products.

From our small town roots, we’ve grown to support mills, truckers, and customers across multiple states.

Our goal is simple: serve people with excellence to secure the right lumber, when needed, at a fair price for all involved in the process.

About Us

People matter more than product or profit.

Founded in 2015 in Kalispell, Montana, Missions Lumber Company was built on the belief that people matter more than product or profit. As a family-run business, we value integrity, relationships, and doing the right thing, even when it’s not the easiest thing.

We support small businesses, deeply value our customers, and treat every partner with respect, honesty, and care.
